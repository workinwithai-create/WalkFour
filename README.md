# WalkFour

Four-bar live acoustic walking-bass desk for unfinished choruses.

Bedroom producers and AI-music finishers already have a vocal and a kit. The low end is a root thud or an 808 slide, so the song never walks. Session players write four live bars — quarter walk, chromatic approach, skip walk, two-feel, turnaround, pedal-then-walk — then you bounce a WAV onto the chorus.

Live samples only. FluidR3 acoustic bass and a soft Rhodes chair from the public GM bank, plus the PreEight kit. No oscillators.

Today's earlier ship was RhodesFour. WalkFour is the missing human figure under the keys.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Copper Room, Late Radio, Yard Light, Night Shift, Porch Wire).
2. Pick a walk (quarter, chromatic approach, skip, two-feel, turnaround, pedal).
3. Hear the hole: the chorus without the walk.
4. Hear the full pass: four bars of hole, then the four-bar walk.
5. Bounce a WAV of the walk and copy the punch list.
